- **Input:** How can a generator hallucinate despite correct retrieval, and what causes it?
- **Actual Output:** - The generator can appear to hallucinate even when retrieval was "correct" because of which documents or instructions it was told to use. If you told the generator to base its answer on certain higher-priority documents that were themselves wrong or the wrong set, the generator will dutifully produce an answer from those documents. In that case it "mixed up the wrong things" (for example, was given six weeks of incorrect data) rather than inventing facts out of thin air.

- Another cause is an expectation mismatch (e.g., you expect output in a particular format). Even with correct context, the generator may not produce the specific output you want if the formatting or output instructions are unclear.

- How to handle it: with generative AI you can see the wrong output and tell the model it's wrong, ask it again, judge and correct it. With agentic (autonomous) systems this is harder because the work is happening without that same interactive correction.

This happens because the retriever may fetch the right documents, but the generator can still misread them, over-prioritize certain text, or add unsupported facts.
